# International Society of Sports Nutrition Position Stand: Long-Chain Omega-3 Polyunsaturated Fatty Acids
PMID 39810703

## Acknowledgments
AcknowledgmentsDedicated to Hector Lopez – Father, Husband, Scientist, and PhysicianThis Position Stand was originally the brainchild of Hector Lopez, MD, a respected physician and scientist. Dr. Lopez was renowned for his groundbreaking work in sports performance and regenerative medicine. His expertise and passion for research and formulation led to significant advancements in these fields. Dr. Lopez’s energy, intellect, and unwavering commitment to improving health outcomes left a lasting legacy in both medicine and nutrition. He was also one of the early pioneers who helped the ISSN establish the sports nutrition field. Hector passed away at the age of 46, and he is deeply missed. This Position Stand is dedicated to his memory and contributions.
