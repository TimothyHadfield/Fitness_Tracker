# Fitness Research

A knowledge library. Research on lifting, nutrition and recovery, summarised into notes that
are readable end to end and traceable back to real papers.

Every claim in here should be followable to a source. Where a source states something the
evidence does not carry, the note says so rather than repeating it.

## Why these sources

They were chosen by measurement, not reputation. Eighteen candidate channels were probed for
catalogue size, video length, caption availability and whether their descriptions carry
resolvable reference links, with Jeff Nippard as a control to check the detector worked. The
selection criteria, the measurements and the sources ruled out — including several that are
excellent but whose terms forbid automated collection — are in
[PROJECT-CONTEXT.md](PROJECT-CONTEXT.md).

The short version: a source earns a place here if you can trace its claims to papers. A large
audience is not a qualification, and several of the biggest names in fitness are deliberately
absent because nothing they publish can be checked.

## How to read it

Each source folder has the same shape:

- **`SUMMARY.md`** — the whole source distilled into one file, organised by the decision
  you're making rather than by video or paper. Start here.
- **Domain summaries** — where a source is large enough to need them.
- **`README.md`** — an index of every note in that folder.
- **`RESEARCH-CITATIONS.md`** — the master bibliography for that source.
- **The notes themselves** — one per video or paper.

## What this is not

These are notes, not transcripts, and not reproductions. Nothing here republishes a source's
text. Where a source is open access, its licence is recorded in the note; where a source is
not, we summarise in our own words and link out.

None of this is medical advice.
