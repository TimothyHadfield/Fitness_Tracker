# Jeff Nippard - video notes

Structured notes on 279 of Jeff Nippard's research and education videos, drawn from the
channel's caption tracks and his own video descriptions. One file per video: the claims,
numbers, rep ranges, study findings and recommendations, with the intros, sponsor reads,
program plugs and filler stripped out.

Every note carries a link back to the original video and a publish date, and - where his
description listed sources - a `## References` section resolving those sources to full
citations with DOIs. **2,075 references are resolved across the library and 726 specific
claims are linked inline to specific papers.** The master bibliography is
[RESEARCH-CITATIONS.md](RESEARCH-CITATIONS.md).

Dates matter here. These videos span **2014 to 2026** and his positions moved a long way over
that decade - most visibly on volume, on stretch-position training, on how strict technique
needs to be, and on reverse dieting. In December 2024 he published an outright retrospective
retracting several of his own past positions
([what science-based lifting gets right](lifting-over.md)). Where an old note and a new note
conflict, the newer one is his current view, and the summaries below say so explicitly rather
than flattening the disagreement.

Half the library is early: **199 of the 279 videos are from 2014-2019**, and the EMG-driven
exercise rankings of that era are the material he has revised most.

These are notes, not transcripts. If you want his exact words, watch the video.
