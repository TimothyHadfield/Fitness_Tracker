# Jeff Nippard — video notes

Fifty of Jeff Nippard's most educational videos, condensed into structured notes.
Each file covers one video: the claims, numbers, rep ranges, study findings and
recommendations, with the intros, sponsor reads, program plugs and filler stripped out.

Every note carries a link back to the original video and a publish date. Dates matter
here — his position on volume in particular has shifted over the years, so a 2018 note
and a 2024 note may not agree. Where they conflict, the newer one is usually his
current view.

These are notes, not transcripts. If you want his exact words, watch the video.
