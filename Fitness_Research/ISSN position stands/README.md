# ISSN position stands

The International Society of Sports Nutrition publishes consensus position stands: multi-author
documents that review a literature and state what the society holds to be true. This folder
covers **26 position stands plus the society's comprehensive review update**, spanning
**2007 to 2026**.

They are the highest-authority source in this library and the cheapest to work with. Every one
is open access under a Creative Commons licence, the full text and the reference list arrive
already structured, and the articles carry their own inline citation markers - so a claim in a
note here can be traced to the specific paper the society relied on, not merely to the stand.

**5,192 unique references** across the 27 documents, 94% of them resolvable to a PubMed ID or
DOI. They are deduplicated in [RESEARCH-CITATIONS.md](RESEARCH-CITATIONS.md), sorted so that a
paper cited by several stands is visibly load-bearing rather than incidental.

## Read this before you trust them

A position stand is a consensus document, and consensus documents have an interest in sounding
confident. Reading all 27 turned up a consistent pattern worth knowing about:

- **The headline position is often firmer than the evidence review that precedes it.** Several
  stands describe a literature as "equivocal" or "largely null" in the body and then assert a
  benefit in the position statement.
- **Authors frequently wrote the studies they are assessing.** That is not a declared conflict
  and not misconduct, but it is a real limit on independence.
- **Declared industry ties are common and sometimes directly material** - an HMB stand written
  in part by someone conducting research for the company that commercialised HMB, an energy
  drink stand whose disclosure statement contradicts its own conflict-of-interest section.
- **Six of the older articles carry no retrievable declaration at all.**

Every note here carries a `Disclosures and funding` section and a `Strength of the evidence`
section for exactly this reason. Read them.

## The revised pairs

Five topics have been revised, and the revisions are the most interesting reading in the
folder - a society changing its mind in public, usually without saying so.

| Topic | First | Revised | What moved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Protein | [2007](protein-2007.md) | [2017](protein-2017.md) | Same 1.4-2.0 g/kg headline; BCAA dosing deleted entirely, timing demoted to "minimal" |
| Creatine | [2007](creatine-2007.md) | [2017](creatine-2017.md) | Loading lengthened, the short-load shortcut dropped, cycling silently abandoned |
| Nutrient timing | [2008](nutrient-timing-2008.md) | [2017](nutrient-timing-2017.md) | The 30-minute anabolic window dissolves; no protein deadline survives |
| Caffeine | [2010](caffeine-2010.md) | [2021](caffeine-2021.md) | Glycogen sparing dismissed; genotype, sleep and delivery form all new |
| Energy drinks | [2013](energy-drinks-2013.md) | [2023](energy-drinks-2023.md) | Guidance for cardiovascular/metabolic disease loosened as the evidence tightened |
| HMB | [2013](hmb-2013.md) | [2025](hmb-2025.md) | Survives as a clinical agent, loses its case as a bodybuilding supplement |
